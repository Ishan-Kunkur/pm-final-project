Arrive

User Action: Opens the app → sees three hand-picked titles for tonight, not 15,000.

Internal State: Curious instead of overwhelmed → the evening starts with a manageable choice.

Pain Point Addressed: Twenty-minute scroll (UXR-01) → replaced by a ready-made shortlist.

Choose

User Action: Reads a short curator note on each pick → understands why it suits her.

Internal State: Confident → commits within minutes instead of second-guessing.

Pain Point Addressed: Option overload (UXR-12) → a bridge pick (“loved X? try Y”) eases her out of the comfort loop.

Watch

User Action: Starts on TV, finishes on tablet → progress and saved picks follow her.

Internal State: Immersed → no friction breaks the evening.

Pain Point Addressed: Lost progress and lost saves (BUG-1058, BUG-1061) → a curated pick survives to completion.

Return

User Action: Rates the film with one tap → tomorrow’s picks get sharper.

Internal State: Looking forward → discovery becomes a habit instead of a chore.

Pain Point Addressed: “Discovery feels like a chore” (UXR-03) → replaced by a reliable nightly ritual.

Control vs. Variant

  • Control (A): Wanderer Home today with the Spotlight rail removed (holdout): StreamLine header with profile avatar, Continue Watching row, existing browse rows [list in order], static bottom nav (Home, Search, My List). No autoplay trailers.
  • Variant (B): Control Home plus one Spotlight rail ("Picked by our curators") with 8 tiles (poster, title, year · runtime), placed directly below Continue Watching. Continue Watching keeps its control position. No "Why?" chips. No autoplay. No changes to any other row, the nav, or the header.

2. Sessions per week: hold at the 1.1 baseline or better, stretch target 1.2 by week 8.

3. Titles completed per week: from [baseline pulled in week 1] to +10% relative by week 8.

  • Bad signal to watch for: Fewer than 35% of Wanderers who open the push or watch the demo start a play within 24 hours, measured at end of week 5.
  • Likely post-launch decision: Expand to Casuals if play-start rate reaches 32% by week 8 and the early-warning signal never fired. Otherwise pause the push and video, fix the landing experience, and re-check within 3 business days.
