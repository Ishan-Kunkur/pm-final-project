| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Spotlight rail on Home |
| Goal | Engagement |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, Wanderers find something to play faster, so fewer of their sessions end without a play.
- **Target audience:** Wanderers

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, Wanderers are 41% of the base and have shown the highest behavioral change in terms of churn. if we are able to convert them and learn from them then we can release it to all personas after hashing all the details out. This will also allow us to learn more on user behaviour and then we can expand it to other personas

## Channels
1. **Owned: In app push notifications**
2. **Earned: Social mentions**
3. **Paid: Paid social influencer Marketing**

## Enablement & Assets
Short form demo video and In app push notifications

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** 1. Play-start rate per Wanderer session: from 29% (assumed baseline, all-user funnel) to 32% by week 8 (primary; a 10% relative lift).
2. Sessions per week: hold at the 1.1 baseline or better, stretch target 1.2 by week 8.
3. Titles completed per week: from [baseline pulled in week 1] to +10% relative by week 8.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Fewer than 35% of Wanderers who open the push or watch the demo start a play within 24 hours, measured at end of week 5.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Expand to Casuals if play-start rate reaches 32% by week 8 and the early-warning signal never fired. Otherwise pause the push and video, fix the landing experience, and re-check within 3 business days.
